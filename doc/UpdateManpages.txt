To recreate the generated part of leapdesk-client manpage use a command like:
  help2man -N --output=leapdesk-client.1 --name='Leapdesk Keyboard/Mouse Client' /path/to/leapdesk-client

And for leapdesk-server:
  help2man -N --output=leapdesk-server.1 --name='Leapdesk Keyboard/Mouse Server' /path/to/leapdesk-server

After that manually fixup:
 * replace your home directory with $HOME in leapdesk-server.1
 * revert formatting of FILES and COPYRIGHT sections to match formatting of existing version.
 * add note referencing this file for instructions to the first line.
